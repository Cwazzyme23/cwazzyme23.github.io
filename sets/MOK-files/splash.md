The march of the machines has finally come to an end. The good people across the multiverse have won a bitter, pyrrhic victory. The moment the ichor lost its power, many innocent people who were victims of compleation simply became dead. The planeswalkers have lost their sparks, and it isn’t coming back. The multiverse is in great disarray. One man, moved by a great loss, is about to make drastic decisions which will leave a plane in disaster. Kaladesh, once the land of great innovation and technological advancements, now finds itself in the grip of a devastating post-war aether drought. The mob presence in the land grows with each passing day, and with a weakened governing body, the consulate struggles to maintain order. Then finally, one day a blast rings out across the plane, and strange sandstorms form in the distance. Those who are even slightly attuned to the blind eternities find their minds scrambled, but once they regain their faculties, they track the source to a large device, assembled behind a building in town in the dead of night. The consulate sends members of The Honorable to investigate, and so does the mob send its own people. Forced to divert their resources away from the investigation to sort the rising mob problems and civil unrest, but suspecting foul play, the consulate is forced to turn to Max Clover: Private Eye to solve what could very well be the single most high-stakes Murder on Kaladesh!
